### Featured projects
 
#### ☕ [METRA](https://github.com/Kenny-vg/METRA-app_web) · SaaS for restaurant & café reservations
*Capstone project · Jan – Apr 2026 · Team of 5 · Project lead & backend developer*
 
A multi-tenant SaaS platform that replaces notebooks, phone calls and WhatsApp with structured reservations, table management and real-time occupancy for cafés and restaurants in Tehuacán. It was designed from surveys and interviews with local businesses.
 
- **Architecture:** decoupled client-server design. A **Laravel REST API** is the core, shared by a web app ([`METRA-app_web`](https://github.com/Kenny-vg/METRA-app_web)) and an Android app for staff ([`METRA-app_movil`](https://github.com/Kenny-vg/METRA-app_movil)).
- **Backend:** token-based authentication with role-based access control (SuperAdmin, manager, staff, customer), normalized **MySQL** schema, SQL views and an analytics controller that computes KPIs such as real occupancy, total visits, average stay and no-show rate.
- **Features:** reservations, zones and tables, walk-ins, digital menu, events and promotions, reviews, automatic email confirmations, subscription plans per business.
- **Quality:** 45 documented test cases (manual, **Selenium**, **Postman**, **OWASP ZAP** for SQL injection) and bug tracking with the QA teammate.
- **Process:** 7 Scrum sprints managed in **Jira**; I planned the sprints, assigned tickets and made the technical decisions.
- **Stack:** Laravel · MySQL · Blade · JavaScript (Axios, Vite) · Tailwind CSS · Kotlin (Android)
#### 🏋️ [Check & Train](https://github.com/Kenny-vg/Check-Train) · Gym membership management
*Capstone project · May – Aug 2025 · Team of 6 · Project lead & developer*
 
A desktop application built for a **real gym client**: it manages clients, memberships and payments, and validates entry with **unique QR tokens** that are emailed to each member. The gym staff were trained to operate it on their own.
 
- Role-based login (manager and receptionist) with passwords hashed using **BCrypt**.
- Client CRUD, membership types and prices, payment registration with automatic expiry dates.
- QR generation and validation that denies access to expired or invalid memberships.
- PDF reports: per client, attendance and cash register summary.
- 45 test cases (77% passed on first run, all issues fixed before delivery).
- **Stack:** Java 21 · Swing (FlatLaf) · MySQL (JDBC) · Maven · ZXing · JavaMail · iText 7 · jBCrypt
